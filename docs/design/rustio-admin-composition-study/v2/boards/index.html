rustio-admin × RustIO composition — study v2

The v1 conclusion stands: rustio-admin adopts RustIO's composition and scale without losing a component. v2 re-checks it against the two RustIO implementation branches that appeared after v1, revises four points, and adds the pages v1 did not draw.

E

Primary colour

Keep #1F5797. Re-evaluated against the current repositories: RustIO PR #5 removed the one page that injected a different blue and re-pinned the scaffold to the contract blue; rustio-admin already carries the same value. The family blue is settled on both sides; a different primary for rustio-admin alone would undo the convergence this study exists for. --focus #2F7BD6 stays distinct from the primary (PR #5 restates why), and AdminTheme already leaves it alone.
