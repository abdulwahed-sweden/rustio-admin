FixShop

A repair shop, run from the admin panel.

Someone brings in a broken thing — a phone, a laptop, a bike, a washing machine. The shop takes it in, works out what is wrong, and sends the customer a price. If the customer says yes, it gets repaired. Then they come back and pick it up.

  • Front desk — books items in, records what the customer said, hands them back.
  • Technician — diagnoses, quotes, repairs.
  • Owner — all of it, plus editing quotes and deleting.

A job cannot move to in progress until its quote is approved.

Sign in as anna (front desk), mike (technician) or sara (owner) — passwords in README.md.
