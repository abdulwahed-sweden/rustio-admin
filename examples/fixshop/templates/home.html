FixShop

A repair shop, run from the admin panel.

An item comes in, gets diagnosed, gets a price. The customer says yes or no. If yes, it gets fixed and picked up.

  1. Front desk books the item in and records what the customer said.
  2. Technician diagnoses, quotes, and repairs.
  3. Owner can do all of it, plus edit quotes and delete.

A job cannot move to in progress until its quote is approved. Jobs past their promised date sit at the top of the list.
