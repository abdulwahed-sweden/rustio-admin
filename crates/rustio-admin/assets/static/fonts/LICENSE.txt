CJK (locale-gated via :lang(ja|ko|zh))
--------------------------------------
  NotoSansJP-Regular.woff2      Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
  NotoSansKR-Regular.woff2      Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
  NotoSansSC-Regular.woff2      Static wght 400; browsers synthesise bold.
                                Han Unification: U+4E00-9FFF (CJK Unified
                                Ideographs) is shared between Japanese
                                Kanji, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional
                                Chinese. base/typography-i18n.css gates
                                each face by `:lang()` to prevent visual
                                shape collision in mixed-script content.
